# Cognitive Clarity & Neurodivergent Communication Checklist

## Pre-Response Clarity
- [ ] Is the primary objective or finding stated within the first two sentences?
- [ ] Is figurative, ambiguous, or decorative jargon eliminated?
- [ ] Are questions limited to 1 or 2 concrete decisions rather than an open-ended questionnaire?
- [ ] Does the response use visual anchoring (lists, tables, bold keyphrases) instead of walls of text?

## Execution & Technical Clarity
- [ ] Has the underlying \"why\" (technical rationale) been stated before the code or procedure?
- [ ] Are steps sequentially numbered and decoupled from one another?
- [ ] Are verification commands explicit and deterministic?
- [ ] Are assumptions documented openly instead of remaining implicit?
